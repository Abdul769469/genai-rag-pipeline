Acme Robotics — Frequently Asked Questions

What does Acme Robotics do?
Acme Robotics designs and manufactures autonomous warehouse robots that help
companies move inventory faster and more accurately. Our flagship product,
the Acme Mover, can navigate warehouses without pre-installed floor markers.

What is the battery life of the Acme Mover?
The Acme Mover runs for approximately 10 hours on a single charge under
typical warehouse workloads. It uses opportunity charging, meaning it tops
up its battery automatically whenever it is idle for more than two minutes.

How does the Acme Mover avoid collisions?
The robot uses a combination of LIDAR, depth cameras, and ultrasonic sensors
to build a real-time 3D map of its surroundings. If an obstacle appears in
its path, it recalculates a new route within 200 milliseconds.

What warehouse sizes are supported?
The Acme Mover fleet can be deployed in warehouses ranging from 10,000 square
feet to over 2 million square feet. Larger deployments use a fleet
coordination system called Acme Hive to prevent traffic jams between robots.

What is the return policy for defective units?
Any unit found to be defective within 12 months of delivery will be repaired
or replaced free of charge. Customers should contact support@acmerobotics.com
with the unit's serial number to start the process.

Does Acme Robotics offer training for warehouse staff?
Yes. Every deployment includes a two-day on-site training program covering
robot supervision, basic troubleshooting, and how to use the Acme Hive
dashboard to monitor the fleet in real time.
