Acme Mover — Technical Specifications

Dimensions and weight
The Acme Mover measures 80cm x 60cm x 40cm and weighs 95kg when unloaded.
Its maximum payload capacity is 500kg on a flat, dry warehouse floor.

Navigation system
Navigation relies on SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping) combined
with a fleet-wide coordination layer. No floor tape, magnets, or QR-code
markers are required for setup.

Speed and maneuverability
Top speed is 2.2 meters per second when carrying a full payload, and up to
3.5 meters per second when empty. The turning radius is 0.9 meters, which
allows operation in standard warehouse aisles as narrow as 1.8 meters.

Connectivity
Each unit connects to the warehouse network over Wi-Fi 6 and reports
telemetry to the Acme Hive dashboard every 500 milliseconds. A cellular
backup connection is available as an optional add-on for facilities with
unreliable Wi-Fi coverage.

Safety certifications
The Acme Mover is certified under ANSI/ITSDF B56.5 for safety standards
of driverless, automatic guided industrial vehicles, and includes an
emergency stop button on all four sides of the chassis.
