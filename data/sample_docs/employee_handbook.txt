Acme Robotics — Employee Handbook & Company Policies

Company history
Acme Robotics was founded in 2016 by a team of former industrial engineers
who believed warehouse automation was too expensive and too rigid for
mid-sized businesses. The company shipped its first commercial unit, the
Acme Mover V1, in 2019. By 2023, Acme Robotics had deployed over 4,000
robots across 300 warehouses in 12 countries. The company is headquartered
in Austin, Texas, with a secondary engineering office in Krakow, Poland.

Remote work policy
Acme Robotics operates a hybrid work model for corporate staff. Employees
are expected to be in the office at least three days per week: Tuesday,
Wednesday, and Thursday. Monday and Friday are flexible remote days.
Engineering and manufacturing staff working directly on the production
floor are not eligible for remote work due to the hands-on nature of
robot assembly and testing.

Vacation and leave policy
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid vacation per year, increasing
to 23 days after three years of tenure. Employees can carry over up to
5 unused vacation days into the following year; anything beyond that is
forfeited. Acme Robotics also offers 10 paid sick days annually and up to
12 weeks of paid parental leave for both primary and secondary caregivers.

Expense reimbursement
Employees traveling for work-related purposes (client site visits,
conferences, training) can submit expenses through the internal Concur
portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Meals are capped at
$75 per day when traveling domestically and $100 per day internationally.
Reimbursement typically takes 5-7 business days after approval.

Equipment and software provisioning
New employees receive a laptop, monitor, and any role-specific hardware
(e.g. safety goggles and a tablet for warehouse technicians) within their
first week. Software licenses are provisioned automatically based on team
membership; requests for additional tools go through the IT ticketing
system and are typically approved within 48 hours.

Performance review cycle
Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Each cycle
includes a self-assessment, manager feedback, and peer feedback from at
least two colleagues. Compensation adjustments and promotions are
typically decided following the December cycle, with changes taking
effect the following February.

Customer support tiers
Acme Robotics offers three support tiers for customers who purchase Acme
Mover fleets. The Standard tier includes email support with a 24-hour
response time and quarterly software updates. The Priority tier adds
phone support with a 4-hour response time and monthly on-site health
checks. The Enterprise tier includes a dedicated account engineer, a
1-hour response time SLA, and access to beta features like predictive
maintenance alerts before they're released to other tiers.

Warranty details
All Acme Mover units include a standard 12-month warranty covering
manufacturing defects in hardware and software. Customers on the
Enterprise support tier receive an extended 36-month warranty at no
additional cost. Wear-and-tear items like wheels and battery packs are
covered for the first 12 months only, after which replacement parts can
be purchased through the Acme Robotics parts portal.

Sustainability initiatives
Acme Robotics committed to carbon-neutral manufacturing by 2027. As of
2024, the Austin manufacturing facility runs on 60% solar power generated
on-site, with the remainder sourced from renewable energy contracts. The
company also runs a robot refurbishment program: units returned after a
customer upgrade are refurbished and resold at a discount rather than
scrapped, which reduced e-waste by an estimated 40 tons in 2023 alone.

Data privacy and security
Telemetry data collected by Acme Mover units (location, battery status,
task logs) is encrypted in transit and at rest, and is retained for 90
days by default unless a customer requests a longer retention period for
analytics purposes. Acme Robotics does not sell customer telemetry data to
third parties. Customers can request full data deletion by contacting
privacy@acmerobotics.com, and requests are processed within 30 days in
compliance with GDPR and CCPA requirements.
